# Block & Re-entry

## Blocking and Re-entry Protocol

**Block & Re-entry** is a personal boundary method for online social relationships involving a repeated **blocking → return** pattern.

It is primarily intended for situations involving **unequal emotional investment, one-sided emotional labor, repeated interruptions of communication, or other forms of emotional exploitation** in romantic, intimate, ambiguous, or friendship-based relationships.

The basic idea is:

> **A person has the right to leave a relationship, but returning does not automatically restore the previous relationship status.**

### Basic Procedure

```text
The other person blocks you
        ↓
Communication is interrupted
        ↓
Do not chase, bypass the block, or monitor their return
        ↓
The other person voluntarily returns
        ↓
Inform them that a re-entry period is required
        ↓
During the re-entry period:
no normal communication / limited participation
        ↓
Re-entry period ends
        ↓
Normal communication can resume
```

The **re-entry period** is not intended as punishment. Its purpose is to introduce a corresponding relationship cost between **leaving and re-entering**, rather than allowing the relationship to follow:

> Block → Return → Immediate restoration of the previous relationship.

This creates a more **symmetric interaction structure**.

### Intended Use

The method may be useful in online social relationships involving:

* emotional exploitation;
* one-sided emotional labor;
* repeated blocking and unblocking;
* using communication withdrawal to make the other person chase;
* persistent asymmetry in relationship investment;
* excessive anxiety caused by uncertainty and repeated waiting or monitoring.

The method does not require determining whether the other person has a particular personality disorder, nor does it require proving malicious intent.

The focus is simply:

> **What happened, and under what conditions am I willing to continue participating in the relationship?**

### Core Principle

**Blocking is not a punishment tool, and the re-entry period is not retaliation.**

It is simply a participation rule:

> **You can leave, and you can come back. But coming back means rebuilding the relationship rather than automatically resetting it.**
